# Cosmetic economy simulation baseline

Run on 2026-08-13 with the checked-in configuration.

## Gacha — 1,000,000 pulls

Observed rates after pity:

- Common: 36.66%
- Uncommon: 27.51%
- Rare: 19.26%
- Epic: 11.21%
- Legendary: 5.35%

The rates differ from raw 40/30/18/9/3 because guaranteed pulls intentionally raise upper-tier outcomes. Pity activated about 46.5k times for Rare+, 21.4k for Epic+, and 16.1k for Legendary.

## Economy — 100,000 simulated 12-week seasons, 8 players

- Average earned balance before spending: 13.88 QP per player.
- 85.86% of player-seasons could afford at least one Rare item.
- 59.98% could afford at least one Epic item.

This is a baseline, not a live-balance promise. Re-run both scripts after changing rewards, prices, odds, or pity.
